Manifesto

Balance, proven.

The question isn't whether to use AI. It's how much, where, and who stays in charge. Three beliefs shape every part of Causvia.

Security & privacy

Built for trust

Operations data is customer data. These are the commitments Causvia is being built around, and we confirm the details with every early-access partner.

Customer data isolation

Each customer's data is kept separate from everyone else's, and your tickets and results are not used to train models for other customers.

On-premise option

When data can't leave your walls, Causvia can be deployed inside your own infrastructure.

Full audit trail of every AI decision

Every proposal, approval, run and Skeptic verdict is logged with who, what, when and why, ready for a manager, an auditor or a regulator.

Designed for India's DPDP Act and GDPR

Consent, purpose limitation and data-subject rights are part of the design from day one, not added later.

Causvia is in early access. Deployment options and data-handling terms are agreed in writing with each partner.
