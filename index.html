About Causvia
About Causvia · The R&D lab for the hybrid workforce

AI flies the routine.
You command the flight.

Causvia finds, by experiment, the right balance between people and AI in every workflow — goal, study, propose, approve, run, doubt, remember — as one continuous loop. AI runs and remembers the experiments. Humans make the calls.

01What Causvia is

Causvia is an AI-human experiment engine for operations teams. Instead of guessing how much work to hand to AI, it tests the split on real workflows and keeps the evidence. Not a chatbot. Not another automation vendor pushing for maximum autonomy. One system that runs disciplined experiments, doubts its own results, and remembers everything it learns.

state the goal → study the workflow → propose an experiment → a human approves → run it against a holdout → the Skeptic attacks the result → remember what was proven
One loop

Not a suite

Seven steps, one continuous cycle. Every experiment feeds the next, so the whole operation learns instead of each team forgetting its own lessons.

Humans in command

Not autopilot

AI proposes; people approve. Decisions about direction, customers, budget and ethics always wait for a human, and autonomy retreats the moment confidence drops.

It shows its proof

Not a pitch

Every recommendation links to the experiment behind it — the data, the holdout, the confidence — or says plainly that it isn’t proven yet.

02Why it exists

Every operations leader is being told to automate. Most vendors measure success by how much work they take away from people, not by whether customers and teams end up better off. Some workflows get worse with more AI; others are held back by too little. Nobody knows the right split, because nobody tests it.

Causvia exists to replace that guesswork with evidence — to find, workflow by workflow, where AI should lead, where people should, and where they work best together, and to prove it before anything changes at scale.

03One loop, seven steps

Not a suite of tools — one engine. Every experiment moves through the same seven steps, and what it learns flows into the next one. Causvia is in early access: the loop is being built with design partners now.

01GoalHumanYour team states the outcome and the limits — target, budget, and what is off-limits.
02StudyAIAI studies the workflow as it runs today — volumes, handle times, quality, and where people and AI each do best.
03ProposeAIAI proposes an experiment: a specific change to the human–AI split, with the expected impact and the risk.
04ApproveHumanA human approval gate. Nothing runs until a person says yes, with the evidence in front of them.
05RunAIThe experiment runs on live work against a holdout group, so the result can be compared fairly.
⟐GuardrailHumanCustomer quality and team wellbeing are watched throughout — cross a limit and the run halts and says why.
06DoubtAIThe Skeptic attacks the result — seasonality, ticket mix, sample size, luck. Only survivors are trusted.
07RememberAIEvery result, failures included, goes into the evidence ledger — a living memory of what works and why.

04The one rule: doubted before trusted

This is the heart of Causvia, and the reason to trust it. A finding only becomes a recommendation after it has survived the Skeptic — and every recommendation carries its evidence and its confidence. If something isn’t proven, Causvia says so.

Real experiments — recommendations come from controlled tests on your own workflows, with holdout groups, not from industry averages.
Real doubt — the Skeptic is independent; only a human can overrule it, and the reason is recorded.
Real gates — anything that changes how customers or people are treated waits for human approval.
Honest empty — a new workspace reads “No experiments yet”, never borrowed benchmarks dressed up as your results.
VerifiedThe result survived every check and is ready for your decision. Not provenThe evidence is too thin to act on — and Causvia tells you why. RejectedIt looked like a win but wasn’t. It stays visible, never hidden.

06Why it holds up

Automation itself is becoming a commodity — any vendor can plug a model into a workflow. Causvia’s defensibility is knowing where it should, built from evidence that compounds over time.

Compounding

Memory that grows

Every experiment, including the failures, is kept in the evidence ledger. The longer Causvia runs, the more it knows about your operation.

Independent

A Skeptic you can’t silence

The Skeptic runs its own checks and can only be overruled by a human, with the reason recorded — so the system can’t quietly lower its own standards.

Integrity

Flags surprises, never overclaims

Unusual results are flagged to a person. Every finding states its confidence, and “not proven” is shown plainly.

Auditable

Evidence is first-class

Every recommendation traces back to the experiment, data and checks behind it — ready for a manager, an auditor or a regulator.
