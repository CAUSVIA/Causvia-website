Neutrality charter

The Causvia Neutrality Charter

The commitments that keep Causvia on the side of the evidence, and of the people doing the work.

Our commitments

  1. 01

    Causvia will never build, sell or resell AI agents.

  2. 02

    Causvia will never take payment from AI vendors that depends on how much work goes to AI.

  3. 03

    Every recommendation Causvia makes links to the evidence behind it, including experiments that failed.

  4. 04

    Causvia measures the wellbeing of the people doing the work alongside cost and quality, and reports both.

  5. 05

    No company can pay for a better result, rating or recommendation.

  6. 06

    If Causvia ever changes this charter, the change will be published with the reason.
