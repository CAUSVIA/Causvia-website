Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Last updated 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AUREXIS AI PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated in India with its registered office in Haryana, which operates Causvia ("Causvia," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit our website (the "Site"), contact us, request a workflow balance audit or early access, or when an organisation uses the Causvia experiment engine and related services (together, the "Services").

Causvia is the R&D lab for the hybrid workforce: it helps operations teams find, by experiment, the right balance between people and AI in each workflow. We hold ourselves to the same standard here as in the product: this policy describes what we actually do today, and clearly marks what applies only once the Services are in use with a customer.

Early access. Causvia is in early access. Today the Site is informational: it has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and all contact happens by email. The Services are provided to a small number of organisations under a written agreement.

2. Information we collect about visitors and contacts

  • Information you send us. When you email us (for example to request a free workflow balance audit, join early access, or subscribe to updates), we receive your email address and anything you include, such as your name, job title, company and details about your workflows.
  • Technical data needed to deliver the Site. Like any website, the servers that deliver the Site receive your IP address, browser type and the page requested. Our hosting provider keeps this in short-lived logs for security and reliability. The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a 3D graphics library from the cdnjs content network, so those providers receive your IP address when your browser fetches those files.
  • No cookies or tracking. The Site does not set cookies, does not use analytics, and does not use advertising or cross-site tracking technologies. If this changes, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Please do not send us sensitive personal data, or personal data about your customers or staff, by email. If an audit needs sample data, we will agree a secure way to share it under a written agreement.

6. Customer data processed through the Services

When an organisation uses Causvia, it connects operational data, for example from a helpdesk, CRM or ticketing tool, so that Causvia can study its workflows, run approved experiments and record the results. This data can include personal data about the organisation's end customers (such as ticket contents and contact details) and its staff (such as handling times, quality scores and workload signals).

  • Customer instructions. We process customer data only to provide the Services to that customer, as set out in our written agreement and data processing terms with them. The customer decides what data is connected, and remains responsible for having a lawful basis and giving any required notices, including to its own employees.
  • Isolation. Each customer's data is kept logically separate from every other customer's data. On-premise deployment inside a customer's own infrastructure is available by agreement.
  • No training for others. We do not use one customer's data to train AI models or to improve results for any other customer.
  • AI providers. Some experiments use third-party AI models, or a customer's own models. Which providers may receive customer data is agreed with each customer in writing, and we will list them as subprocessors before any customer data is sent to them.
  • People stay in command. Causvia proposes experiments and recommendations; a person at the customer approves any change before it runs. Causvia is not designed to make decisions about individual employees, and customers must not use it for hiring, firing, disciplinary or pay decisions about individuals.
  • Audit trail. Proposals, approvals, experiment runs and Skeptic verdicts are logged so the customer can review who decided what, and when.
  • Return and deletion. When a customer agreement ends, we return or delete customer data as that agreement specifies.

If you are an end customer or employee of one of our customers and want to exercise your data rights, please contact that organisation directly. If you contact us, we will pass your request to them and help them respond.

7. International transfers

Causvia is operated from Haryana, India. Some of our service providers are located outside India, mainly in the United States. Where personal data is transferred across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the provider's contractual data-protection commitments, and we follow any restrictions notified by the Government of India under the DPDP Act. For EU/UK personal data we use standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards. Where a customer requires its data to stay in a particular country, we agree that in writing, including through on-premise deployment.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access limited to people who need it, and careful selection of providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We do not claim any security certification (such as SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA) unless and until we have actually obtained it.

Personal-data breach. If we become aware of a breach affecting personal data for which we are the Data Fiduciary, we will notify affected people and the Data Protection Board of India (and any other required authority) as the DPDP Act and its rules require, explaining what happened, the likely consequences and the steps taken. For customer data, we will notify the affected customer without undue delay so it can meet its own obligations.
