§01 · What Causvia is

An experiment engine for teams where people and AI share the work.

It tests the human–AI split on your real workflows and keeps the evidence. Not a chatbot. Not an automation vendor pushing for maximum autonomy.

Why it exists

Every operations leader is told to automate. Vendors count work taken from people, not outcomes. Some workflows get worse with more AI; others need more.

Nobody knows the right split, because nobody tests it.

§02 · The loop

Seven steps. Always orbiting.

Every experiment moves through the same loop, and what it learns feeds the next. Gold steps are owned by people; teal steps are run by AI.

Holdout

§03 · The one rule

Doubted before trusted.

The Skeptic attacks every result

Seasonality, ticket mix, sample size, luck. Only survivors become recommendations, each with its evidence and confidence. Only a human can overrule the Skeptic, and the reason is recorded.

● Verified

Survived every check. Ready for your decision.

● Not proven

Too thin to act on, and Causvia says why.

● Rejected

Looked like a win but wasn't. Kept visible.
